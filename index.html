Seven classes, one timetable

Every class is coach-led. Here’s what each one involves, from Elevate’s own class guide.

  • HYROX

    A longer, sweaty conditioning session to build a solid cardio base, based around ergs, running and stations.

  • Strength

    Learn and progress specific lifts over a 6–8 week block, lifting safely and effectively.

  • Met-Con

    A sweaty paired workout: dumbbells, ergs, running, rigs or box jumps.

  • Skills

    Olympic lifting such as power cleans, squat cleans and snatches, plus gymnastics like toes to bar and handstand walks.

  • Engine

    Solo, pairs or teams. High volume, less resistance — no weights or skill, just pure grit and determination.

  • Squad Saturday

    A high-paced, team-of-three conditioning session mixing resistance and bodyweight exercises.

  • Sunday WOD

    Teams of two, smaller blocks with little rest, usually with a barbell — a test of strength and conditioning.
